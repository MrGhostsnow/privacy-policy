Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

FlexReviews ("we", "our", or "us") is a Shopify app that displays product reviews from third-party review platforms on your storefront. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

1. What data we collect

When you install FlexReviews, we collect and store the following:

  • Shop domain — your Shopify store's myshopify.com domain, used to identify your account.
  • Shopify access token — the token Shopify issues to the app when you install it, used to read your plan and to authenticate the app.
  • Judge.me API token — your private API token, used to sync reviews from your Judge.me account. Stored encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM).
  • Review data — reviews synced from your Judge.me account: reviewer name (as provided by Judge.me), rating, title, review text, photo URLs, review date, the product the review belongs to, and verified-buyer status.
  • Reviewer identifiers — when Judge.me provides them, the reviewer's Shopify customer ID and a one-way SHA-256 hash of the reviewer's email address. We use them only to find and delete a customer's reviews when Shopify sends a data deletion request. We never store the email address itself.
  • App configuration — your plan (Free or Pro) and sync interval preference.

2. What data we do not collect

  • We do not collect data from your store's customers directly.
  • We do not collect payment information.
  • We do not collect browsing behavior or analytics from your storefront visitors.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. How we use your data

  • To sync reviews from your Judge.me account and display them on your storefront.
  • To apply your layout and styling preferences to the review display block.
  • To manage your subscription plan and app settings.

4. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on servers hosted by Railway (railway.app), located in the United States. We use industry-standard security practices including encrypted connections (HTTPS), request authentication via HMAC signatures, and JWT-based session verification for all admin operations.

Your Judge.me API token is stored encrypted in our database and used solely to sync review data on your behalf. It is never shared with third parties.

5. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as FlexReviews is installed on your store. Reviews that are deleted or unpublished in Judge.me are removed from our database at the next sync. If you disconnect Judge.me, syncing stops and the reviews already synced stay in your account until you uninstall the app.

When you uninstall the app, syncing stops immediately. Shopify then sends us a shop data deletion request (about 48 hours after uninstalling), and we delete all data associated with your store when we receive it, in accordance with Shopify's app requirements.

6. GDPR and data subject rights

FlexReviews complies with Shopify's mandatory GDPR webhook requirements. When Shopify sends a customer data deletion request, we delete that customer's reviews from our database and stop importing them. When Shopify sends a customer data request, we identify the reviews we hold for that customer; the reviews themselves are owned by your Judge.me account, and Judge.me handles requests for that data.

To submit a data request, contact us at the email address below.

7. Third-party services

FlexReviews integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Judge.me — we access your review data via the Judge.me API on your behalf. Judge.me's own privacy policy applies to how they handle your review data: judge.me/privacy.
  • Shopify — FlexReviews operates within the Shopify platform. Shopify's privacy policy applies to platform-level data: shopify.com/legal/privacy.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of FlexReviews after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to submit a data request, please contact us at: flexreviews.app@gmail.com
